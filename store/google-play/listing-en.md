## Full description (max 4000 characters)
FormLex prepares the legal documents you need, under the law of your country, and our team reviews every draft before it reaches you.

Choose a document, answer a few guided questions, pay once, and receive a reviewed document you can download as PDF or Word. No law firm, no subscription.

WHAT YOU CAN PREPARE
• Tenancy and commercial lease agreements
• Employment contracts, warning and termination letters
• Loan agreements and debt repayment plans
• Non-disclosure, service, consultancy and supply agreements
• Land and property sale agreements, power of attorney
• Letters of demand, settlement agreements, affidavits
• Company documents: shareholders' agreements, board resolutions, partnership agreements
• Can't find your document? Describe what you need and we will draft it.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Choose your document and the country whose law should govern it.
2. Answer simple guided questions. Your answers are saved as you go.
3. Check your answers and pay once by card or mobile money.
4. Our team reviews the draft before it is released to you.
5. Download PDF or Word, or request changes if something needs updating.

BUILT FOR YOUR COUNTRY
Drafts follow the terminology, legal tradition and courts of the country you choose — across Africa, the Middle East, Latin America, Europe and North America — in English or the local language where available.

EVERYTHING IN ONE PLACE
• Follow each document from payment to review to release.
• See every payment and download an invoice for your records.
• Ask our support team for help from inside the app.
• Sign in in seconds with a code on WhatsApp, or with email.
• Use the app in English, Español, Português, Français or العربية.

IMPORTANT
FormLex is a document drafting and review service, not a law firm, and does not provide legal advice. For important, high-value or disputed matters, also consult a lawyer qualified in your country before you sign.
